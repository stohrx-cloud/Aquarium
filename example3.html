About Boffo's

More than a deli. It's your neighbourhood table.

Boffo's Fine Foods has been a staple in Oakville for over a decade. We believe in simple, local ingredients and putting great meals on your table. Quality versus quantity — that's our mantra, and we never compromise a dish's integrity with additives or preservatives.

"Rest easy knowing that all of the fresh meats at Boffo's are selected from local farms, where animals are treated humanely, live free range, without any use of antibiotics or hormones."

From the butcher counter to our prepared meal fridges, everything at Boffo's is made with a deep respect for the ingredients — and for you.

Meet the Owner

Two decades of craft, community, and a whole lot of love for good food.

When Ugo & Margherita first opened up shop in Toronto as the Cabbage Town Butcher Shop, they built on family and great staff. Now in Oakville, Boffo's continues those values under Noel Lourenco and Heather McGregor.

Under chef Noel's guidance — part of the Boffo's family since 2000 — we promise to keep producing tantalizing, local, seasonal dishes unlike anything else in the GTA. When you walk through our doors, you'll taste the difference quality makes.
