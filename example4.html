About Boffo's

Quality over quantity — always.

Boffo's Fine Foods has been a staple in Oakville for over a decade. We believe in simple, local ingredients and putting great meals on your table. We never compromise a dish's integrity with additives or preservatives.

Every cut of meat is selected from local farms where animals are treated humanely, live free range, without any use of antibiotics or hormones. Taste the difference quality makes.

Meet the Owner

Built on family. Driven by flavour. Home in Oakville.

When Ugo & Margherita first opened the Cabbage Town Butcher Shop in Toronto, they built something special on family and great staff. That same spirit lives on in Oakville under Noel Lourenco and Heather McGregor.

Under chef Noel — part of the Boffo's family since 2000 — we promise tantalizing, local, seasonal dishes unlike anything else in the GTA. Walk through our doors and taste the difference quality makes.
