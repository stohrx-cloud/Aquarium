Brew Guides

However you make it at home, make it right.

Three ways to brew our beans, straight from our own counter.

01
Method

French Press

GrindCoarse
Ratio1:15
Time4 minutes
  1. Add coarsely ground coffee to the empty press.
  2. Pour hot water (just off the boil) evenly over the grounds.
  3. Stir gently once, then set the lid on top without pressing.
  4. Steep for 4 minutes.
  5. Press the plunger down slowly and pour immediately.
02
Method

Chemex

GrindMedium-coarse
Ratio1:16
Time~4.5 minutes
  1. Fold the filter and rinse it with hot water; discard the rinse.
  2. Add the ground coffee and level the bed.
  3. Bloom with a small pour, let it sit for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in slow, steady circles until you reach your target weight.
  5. Let it fully drain, then remove the filter and serve.
03
Method

Moka Pot

GrindFine
RatioFill basket
Time4–5 minutes
  1. Fill the bottom chamber with hot water up to the valve.
  2. Fill the funnel basket with fine coffee, level (don't tamp).
  3. Assemble and set on medium heat with the lid open.
  4. Listen for the gurgle — that's your cue it's nearly done.
  5. Remove from heat as soon as the flow turns pale and hissy.
