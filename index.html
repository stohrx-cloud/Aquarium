Our Shop

Oakville's aquarium & aquascaping specialists since 2019

Planted Aquaria was built to bring real aquascaping expertise to Oakville — no big-box guesswork. Every tank in the shop is planted, stocked, and watched by people who keep aquariums themselves.

We carry premium live aquatic plants, healthy tropical fish, and the hardscape, lighting, and CO2 equipment to support a proper planted setup, held to the same standard we'd want for our own tanks at home.

Come in, tell us what you're building, and we'll help you find the right plants, livestock, and gear for it.
