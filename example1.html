Meet the Owner

When Ugo & Margherita first opened up shop in Toronto as the Cabbage Town Butcher Shop, they built their business on family and great staff. Now in Oakville, Boffo's continues those same values under owners Noel Lourenco and Heather McGregor.

Under chef Noel's guidance — part of the Boffo's family since 2000 — we promise to keep producing tantalizing, local, seasonal dishes you won't find anywhere else in the GTA.
