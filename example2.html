Who We Are

"Quality versus quantity — every single time."

Boffo's Fine Foods has been a staple in Oakville for over a decade. We believe in simple, local ingredients and putting great meals on your table. Every cut of meat is selected from local farms where animals are raised humanely, free range, without antibiotics or hormones. Come taste the difference.

Meet the Owner

Over two decades of passion on a plate.

When Ugo & Margherita first opened the Cabbage Town Butcher Shop in Toronto, they built something special on family and great staff. That same spirit lives on in Oakville under Noel Lourenco and Heather McGregor.

Under chef Noel's guidance — part of the Boffo's family since 2000 — we promise to keep producing tantalizing, local, seasonal dishes unlike anything else in the GTA.
