        nuestra historia
        
    Coffee, food, music and warm, friendly people
    
        
    Yury Wu-Parra opened Taste of Colombia in 2012 to share the best of home with Canada. In 2014 we grew into El Salón next door, and it quickly filled up with salsa, Spanish conversation, art and wellness classes.
    
        
    In 2026 our neighbours voted us the Customer Loyalty & Community Favourite at the first Bronte Business Impact Awards. Thank you, Bronte.
